Home for Northeast India, inside SRCC.

The Northeast Cell brings together students from Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura, Arunachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Ladakh, Darjeeling, and Kalimpong — building community, culture, and a stronger voice on campus.

What we do

Three things, done well.

Community

Belonging on campus

A first point of contact for NE students arriving at SRCC — mentorship, orientation support, and a peer network from day one.

Culture

Cultural showcase

Flagship cultural events that bring Northeast India's food, music, and traditions to the wider SRCC and DU community.

Network

Inter-college reach

Building ties with Northeast cells and societies across Delhi University and beyond.
